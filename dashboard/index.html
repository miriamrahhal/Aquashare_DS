What is my share?
Every morning the water authority gives each farm a share of the reservoir. You can use it, or give part of it to a neighbour.
What if my internet goes down?
Keep working. Your farm keeps watering from your share and remembers everything. It sends the updates by itself when the connection is back.
Can I cancel a transfer?
No. Once a neighbour receives water, it is theirs. That is why we ask you to confirm before sending.
What does “Verify” do?
It checks, with a cryptographic proof, that your transfer is really recorded on all servers, exactly as shown. It is your receipt.

How AquaShare works

The whole idea in four steps
1
The day opensThe ledger releases the reservoir’s daily water and gives each farm its share.
2
Farms irrigateEach farm spends only its own share, so it keeps working even without network.
3
Farms share waterA farm with spare water can send some of it to another farm.
4
The ledger keeps scoreEvery use and transfer is saved on 3 servers, so nothing is lost or counted twice.
